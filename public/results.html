ℹ️ How Voting is Collected & Stored

1. In-App Digital Scorecard

Attendees rate appearance, aroma, flavor, texture, and overall score (1–5 stars) on the app's ballot screen with booth lookup.

2. Cloud Storage & Sync

Votes are sent instantly to our Supabase database (`ballot_votes`) with local offline caching (`jj_tasting_ballot_v2`) for fail-safe tallying.

3. Live Organizer Tally

This dashboard updates live and aggregates scores in real-time. Organizers can export full CSV tallies before the 3:30 PM awards ceremony.
