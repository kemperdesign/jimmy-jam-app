Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Jimmy Jam BBQ Slam app and website (operated by Jimmy Jam Community Outreach Inc., a nonprofit organization) handle your information. We built this app to help you enjoy the Jimmy Jam BBQ Slam event and, where possible, to help connect people in need with assistance — so we try to collect as little personal information as we reasonably can.

Information You Provide Directly

If you submit an Apply for Help assistance application, we collect your first name, last name, email address, phone number, the type(s) of assistance you're requesting, and any description you choose to provide. This information is used only to review and respond to your request for assistance. It is reviewed by authorized Jimmy Jam Community Outreach staff and is never sold or shared with third parties, except as necessary to directly help fulfill your request for assistance.

Location Information

If you use the GPS Map feature to navigate the event grounds, the app accesses your device's location to show your position on the map and give you walking directions to areas like parking, food trucks, and attractions. This location data is used only on your own device while you're using that feature — it is not sent to or stored on our servers.

Device Orientation / Compass

The GPS Map's walking-direction arrow uses your device's compass/orientation sensor, with your permission, to point you the right way. This data is used only locally on your device and is never transmitted or stored.

Ticket and Event Registration Purchases

When you tap "Buy Tickets" or any event registration link, you leave the app and are taken to JotForm, a third-party form and payment provider, to complete your purchase or registration. We do not receive, process, or store your payment card information. Those transactions are subject to JotForm's own privacy policy.

Staff Accounts

A small number of authorized Jimmy Jam Community Outreach staff members have login accounts used only to manage the GPS map pins shown to event visitors. This is an internal tool, not a public account system — the general public does not need, and cannot create, an account to use the app.

Analytics and Advertising

This app does not use third-party analytics or advertising tracking of any kind.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Retention and Deletion

If you'd like us to delete an assistance application you submitted, or have any other question about your information, contact us at jimmyjaminfo@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent changes.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or about the Jimmy Jam BBQ Slam event can be sent to jimmyjaminfo@gmail.com.
