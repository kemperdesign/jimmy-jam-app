## My top 10 recommendations

If we want the app to feel impressive without overbuilding it, I would add these first:

1. People’s Choice Voting
2. BBQ Team Profiles
3. Interactive Event Map
4. BBQ Turn-In Timer
5. Judge Scoring Portal
6. Sponsor Scavenger Hunt
7. Bourbon & BBQ Pairing Recommender
8. BBQ Bingo
9. Live Announcements
10. Sponsor a Family Donation Cards

The most valuable feature combination would be:

```text
People’s Choice Voting + Team Profiles + Event Map + Sponsor Scavenger Hunt + Live Announcements
```

That would make the app feel like a real event companion, not just an informational website.
